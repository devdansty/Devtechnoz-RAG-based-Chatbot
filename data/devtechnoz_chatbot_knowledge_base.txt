COMPANY
DevTechNoz creates digital experiences and business tools for startups, small
businesses, and growing teams. The company is based in Lahore and serves
businesses in the UAE, UK, USA, Canada, and Saudi Arabia. Its focus is combining
strategy, design, and technology to help businesses launch, grow, and improve.

SERVICES
We provide six services:
- Website development: websites designed to engage visitors, represent a
  business, and support its goals; the team works from start to finish.
- Mobile app development: apps for Android and iOS, designed for performance,
  growth, and user experience.
- Business automation: ERP and CRM systems that automate workflows, centralize
  business data, and reduce reliance on spreadsheets.
- UI/UX design: human-focused interfaces designed to be clear and easy to use.
- Digital marketing: data-driven marketing campaigns, including SEO and ads.
- Graphic design and branding: visual design, logos, and complete brand kits.

When asked what services we provide, name these six directly. Do not describe
the way services are grouped in the site navigation or footer.

HOW WE WORK
We focus on understanding a client's goals before choosing a solution. Our
approach combines business strategy, design, and technology. We build around
the client's workflows and goals rather than expecting the client to adapt to
a fixed system. The company describes its communication as clear, fast, and
human, and says it continues supporting clients after launch.

COMPANY VALUES
We treat a client's vision as a partnership and give projects founder-level
focus. We combine design and strategy to create useful results. We build
adaptable technology intended to grow with a business, including automation
and AI-integrated platforms.
